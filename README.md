## I build systems for the parts people usually ignore.

Security. Trust. Abuse. Failure. Risk. Infrastructure.

I’m Daniel Kilonzi, a cybersecurity-focused builder from Kenya working across backend engineering, AI infrastructure, cloud systems, fintech risk, and experimental protocols.

I like the uncomfortable layer beneath real products — the part where systems get attacked, abused, overloaded, misused, misconfigured, or forced to prove they can be trusted.

Most of my work sits around one question:

> How do you build systems that still make sense when the real world gets messy?

---

## What I care about

I’m interested in systems that are:

- secure by design
- hard to abuse
- observable under pressure
- resilient when things fail
- simple enough to operate
- strong enough to survive production

I don’t just want to build dashboards and CRUD apps.

I want to build the infrastructure behind trust.

## The kind of problems I like

I like problems where the answer is not just another dashboard.

I’m drawn to systems that need to answer hard questions like:

- Can this system prove what happened?
- Can it survive abuse?
- Can it detect suspicious behavior early?
- Can it remain understandable under pressure?
- Can it be trusted when money, identity, or infrastructure is involved?
- Can we make the underlying protocol smarter, safer, or more resilient?

That’s the kind of engineering I care about.
